- Agent / assignment: Emerging Technology Disruption Agent, Week 4 Assignment 2.
- General ET finding: Agentic AI can be disruptive where an incumbent business model monetizes scarce human research, analysis, and document production. The general capability does not make every application disruptive; disruption depends on the business model touched by the application.
- Application finding: In management consulting research and deck production, agentic AI directly attacks junior analyst work, billable-hour leverage, cycle time, and first-pass analysis. It creates faster and lower-cost advisory delivery while commoditizing some work that firms historically staffed with junior consultants.
- Organization-specific finding: For the fictional mid-sized strategy consultancy Northstar Advisory, the potency rating is High over an 18-36 month horizon. The recommended management response is to act now through controlled pilots, pricing-model redesign, knowledge-base cleanup, and planned cannibalization of some analyst-hour economics.
- Primary test result: The primary case produced a high-disruption, mid-term threat judgment for a consulting firm whose profit model depends on analyst utilization and project-team billing.
- Contrast result - what stayed stable: The general ET finding stayed stable. Agentic AI still has multi-step knowledge-work capability and can change research, analysis, and drafting workflows.
- Contrast result - what changed: In the hospital administrative strategy-office contrast, the business-model disruption potency fell to Moderate for administrative workflows and Low for the core hospital business model. The recommended action changed from act now on business-model redesign to prepare and pilot under strict governance.
- Weakness/failure found: The first draft of the specialist logic risked labeling agentic AI as disruptive based on the technology label alone. It did not force the agent to establish the business-model baseline before judging disruption.
- Revision made: Added explicit requirements for a business-model baseline, sustaining-versus-disruptive mechanism, creative-destruction ledger, Business-Model Disruption Potency rating, severity/time-horizon placement, incumbent-dilemma analysis, and a trigger for urgency escalation.
- Remaining limitation: The primary organization is fictional, so the output cannot quantify exact financial exposure without actual utilization, margin, pricing, client-retention, and competitor data.
- Independent judgment: Agentic AI is a high creative-destruction threat and opportunity for a consulting firm built on leveraged junior labor. It should be treated as a business-model redesign issue now, not merely as a productivity tool. The same technology is far less disruptive when used only for internal hospital administrative support because it does not directly replace the core care-delivery model.
